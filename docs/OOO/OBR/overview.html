Overview — what OOORF bundles

Oscuro's Oblivion Overhaul Remastered FULL (OOORF) is the Oblivion Remastered (UE5) re-release of the long-running Oscuro's Oblivion Overhaul (OOO) gameplay mod, with Shivering Isles content extended to match.

Note: OOORF (this site) is the FULL edition. The acronym OOORS refers to the separate Slim edition.

What's new in alpha92

alpha92 requires a UE4SS update. Install "UE4SS new" (v3.0.1-1164) from the UE4SS Nexus page (under Update files) before you install alpha92. "UE4SS stable" (3.0.1.a) is no longer supported. See Install for the upgrade steps.

  • 21 new item sets: Ancestral, BladesElite, DaedricPrince, DarkMalachite, Draconis, Dragonborne, Dread, DremoraElite, DurableSteel, ImagoStorm, Light of Dawn (one sword in four restoration stages), Noble, TheBear, UnfinishedAncestral, UnfinishedSteel, UnfinishedWornSteel, WornAncestral, and WornSteel. They add a Steel family, a Daedric family, and an Amelion family to the earlier Glass / Mithril / Elven / Leather / Fur sets. Light of Dawn is the first weapon set beyond the Obsidian line: one sword in four restoration stages: tainted, an initial restoration stage, an intermediate restoration stage, and fully restored at 300 vampire kills. The New Items page lists all 36 sets.
  • ~120 SyncMap rebindings: about 120 OOO items that borrowed a vanilla mesh in alpha91 now point at their own set — for example Dread armor (was Daedric), Noble (was Ebony), Durable Steel (was Steel), the Ancestral sets (were Amelion), Daedric Prince (was Daedric and Dremora), Vella's armor (now BlueGlass and DarkMalachite), the Light of Dawn swords (were Rugdumph's sword), and the enchanted RGlass greaves (now Drakefired greaves).
  • 5 item sets rebuilt: Aureus, Drakefired, Eboron, GrayFox, and Obsidian.
  • All classic OOO dungeons are now complete. Gloom Way Cave (two zones) and Red Gill Cave, the last two, are finished, with their water approved in-game. L_GloomWayCaveXX_Map and L_RedGillCave_Map are rebuilt, with a net +30 ghost-suppression entries baked into the paks.
  • Begone and OOO_REFRFix are now optional test mods. RAX is the only UE4SS mod installed by default. The other two ship under OblivionRemastered\alpha92_test_optional\, and Begone is now log-only. Installing over alpha91 does not remove the old Mods\Begone\ and Mods\OOO_REFRFix\ folders; delete them unless you want them.
  • ESP and MagicLoader fixes: 12 greaves records now use a greaves model instead of boots or gauntlets; the six Harbinger armor pieces are renamed "Ancient Harbinger"; the Jephre's summon names are now localized.

See the alpha92 changelog for the full file-level diff, or the GitHub release page to download.

For the story behind these changes, see the devlog.

Previously: what was new in alpha90

Curated highlights from the prior release — preserved here so the per-release stories accumulate into the eventual beta and GA release notes. See the alpha90 changelog for the full file-level diff.

  • New dungeon map: Lipsand Tarn 03 (L_LipsTarn03_Map.{pak,ucas,utoc}) joined the bundled map set.
  • First three custom item sets debut: Obsidian (bronze trim, black body, by SquigM), BGlass (gold trim, blue body — also SquigM's GoldBlueGlassArmourWeapons, first OOORF conversion), and RGlass (the Drakefired set — a mashup of ProhagonCreations Blackened Glass with the glass parts recolored dark red). Six armor pieces each (Boots, Cuirass, Gauntlets, Greaves, Helmet, Shield). The Obsidian set also introduced a full 9-weapon line for the first time (BattleAxe, Bow, Claymore, Dagger, Longsword, Mace, Shortsword, WarAxe, WarHammer), distributed via a new loot leveled list (LL0LootWeapon7Obsidian100) and folded into two existing NPC bow lists for the Obsidian Bow. In alpha91 the BGlass and RGlass paks were renamed to BlueGlass and Drakefired; the underlying items and visuals carry over.
  • All 16 dungeon map paks rebuilt — ghost-object suppression moved into the paks. Earlier OOORF builds relied on the Begone UE4SS Lua mod to hide UE5-only kit props (extra doors, ambient fires, debris, dynamic FX) at runtime every cell load. Alpha90 migrated that suppression into the map paks themselves: every bundled dungeon — L_Arondar_Map, L_Baras_Map, L_BloodClotCave_Map, L_BrokenToothCaveXX_Map, L_Cehan_Map, L_DeepCoverCav_Map, L_Dostares_Map, L_DrownedHopesCa_Map, L_GloomWayCaveXX_Map, L_GraveGroundCaveXX_Map, L_NarindXXX_Map, L_RedGillCave_Map, L_RosulasXX_Map, L_ThunderingSte_Map, L_VarastalXX_Map, L_Yele_Map — shipped with a refreshed .ucas/.utoc where the suppression is baked in. Result: less Lua work at every cell load, fewer runtime races between Begone and the engine, and a more stable first-entry experience.
  • UE4SS mod updates aligned with the pak migration: the Begone config JSON shrank from ~115 KB → ~60 KB (entries the paks now handle directly removed), while Begone/Scripts/main.lua grew from ~25 KB → ~37 KB to support the new pak-aware suppression flow. OOO_REFRFix/Scripts/main.lua was also refreshed.
  • Data refresh: main ESP, SyncMap INI (incl. Deluxe variant), CELLMAP and WEAP MagicLoader JSONs.

Custom cloned and retextured item sets

OOO has shipped many recolored Glass / Mithril / Elven / Leather / Fur armor families for a long time — their TES4 records, stats, and leveled-list placement all live in the main ESP. What was missing in OOORF builds before alpha90 was their dedicated UE5 appearance: the SyncMap (the authoritative FormID → UE5 asset binding in Oblivion Remastered) used to point all of these items at the vanilla UE5 assets they were originally cloned from, so they rendered as ordinary Glass / Mithril / Elven / Leather / Fur in-game despite the rebalanced stats. Alpha90 introduced this rebinding for Obsidian + BGlass + RGlass; alpha91 expanded it to 14 more sets and renamed the two armor-only Glass-clones from alpha90 to clearer player-facing names (BGlass → BlueGlass, RGlass → Drakefired); alpha92 adds 21 more, including new Steel, Daedric, and Amelion families and the first Light of Dawn sword variants.

Each set ships as a matching <Set>Items + <Set>Materials pak pair, produced by the OblivionRemastered_ItemClone pipeline. See the New Items page → for the full per-set tables (every item's inventory icon, EDID, FormID, and UE5 form path). Credits. Four sets are created from other creators' mods:

  • Obsidian: New items created from the Glass replacer Bronze-Black Glass Armour and Weapons by SquigM.
  • BlueGlass: New items created from the Glass replacer Gold-Blue Glass Armour and Weapons by SquigM.
  • Drakefired: New items using Red glass mod of Blackened Steel Glass Armor and Weapons replacer by ProhagonCreations.
  • DarkMalachite: New items created from the Glass replacer Blackened Steel Glass Armor and Weapons by ProhagonCreations. DarkMalachite uses its textures on the vanilla Glass material.

Stats note: all sets have stats significantly rebalanced compared to their vanilla base material — they are distinct items, not just retextures. Slot coverage and leveled-list placement are auditable in the main ESP with TES4Edit / xEdit if you want the specifics.

UE4SS Lua mods (OblivionRemastered/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/)

Required for normal play

Mod What it does
RAX/ Allows loading of new custom maps. Created by Antrix to allow OOO to load custom UE5 maps. Native DLL helper (dlls/main.dll) plus Lua glue. The alpha92 DLL is built for "UE4SS new" v3.0.1-1164; do not use it with any other UE4SS version. Required.

Optional test mods (OblivionRemastered/alpha92_test_optional/)

Not required for normal play, and from alpha92 not installed by default. These exist to support the OOORF alpha-testing workflow. They ship with their install tree intact; to use one, copy its folder from alpha92_test_optional\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\ into …\Binaries\Win64\ue4ss\Mods\.

Mod What it does
Begone/ Test recording and debugging. Originally Begone suppressed UE5 ghost objects (extra doors, fires, debris, dynamic FX) at runtime via per-cell rules in Config/OscurosOblivionOverhaul.json; as of alpha90 that suppression is baked into the map paks themselves. From alpha92 Begone is log-only: it suppresses nothing. Its remaining use is capturing per-cell object inventories during dungeon walkthroughs and checking suppression coverage when new map paks are built.
OOO_REFRFix/ Save-file migration helper. Fixes spawn / placement deltas for OOO references stored in your save file (Scripts/spawn_deltas.lua). Only required if you have existing saves with older alpha-release OOO references; not needed for new saves or if you never used prior alpha releases.
