New Items — 36 new armor and weapon sets

OOORF gives Oscuro's armor and weapons their own art in Oblivion Remastered. Items that used to look like ordinary vanilla gear now each have a unique look. Some sets, like the Ancestral armor, are completely new in OOORF since they did not exist in classic OOO. Pick a set below to see its pieces, inventory icons, and an in-game screenshot. For modders, each item also lists its EditorID, FormID, and UE5 asset path. Obsidian weapons are not pictured separately but are featured in many of the armor shots. More content that is new in OOORF is listed under Other new content.
