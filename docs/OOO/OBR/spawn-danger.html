Exterior cells are colored squares; dungeons are circles at their entrance, sized by spawn points. Computed from the leveled lists with each creature's level offset, calc-min and calc-max applied, with RAX levlist on. Not measured in game.

Reading it

A roll is one spawn of one list; a pack of three counts as three creatures. "In the place" treats every spawn point in the cell or dungeon as rolled once, which is what a player walking through meets. "Graded" adds up how many levels above the player each creature is (a pack of three creatures 3 levels above counts 9), so a creature fades out over several levels instead of crossing a line. Points disabled at load (enabled later by quests) are left out. Hover for the lists behind a place.
